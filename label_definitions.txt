rule_question: asks what the rules or procedure are, in a way an official source (USCIS, the regulations, a DSO) could answer completely
experience_request: asks other people for their outcomes, timelines or data points, without asking whether the poster's own plan is wise
risk_check: asks for a judgment on whether the poster's own situation or plan is safe, risky, or likely to work out
miscellaneous: does not ask the community a rule, experience or risk question about F-1 status; it shares news, an experience report or feelings, speculates, or asks about something outside F-1 rules
